INTRODUCTION
------------
This Risk Disclosure Statement is prepared in accordance with applicable
regulatory requirements and is intended to provide stakeholders with a
comprehensive overview of the material risks facing Initech Ltd and the
risk management framework employed to identify, assess, and mitigate
such risks.

Initech Ltd is a mid-cap financial services company specializing in
retail lending, micro-finance, and digital banking solutions. The company
operates primarily in India with a network of 850 branches across
18 states and 3 union territories.

RISK MANAGEMENT FRAMEWORK
--------------------------
Initech's risk management framework is structured around three lines of
defence:

First Line: Business units are responsible for identifying and managing
risks in their day-to-day operations. Each business unit has a designated
risk champion who reports to the unit head.

Second Line: The centralized Risk Management Department, headed by the
Chief Risk Officer (CRO), sets risk policies, defines risk appetite,
monitors risk exposures, and provides independent oversight. The CRO
reports directly to the Board Risk Management Committee.

Third Line: Internal Audit provides independent assurance on the
effectiveness of risk management processes and controls.

The Board Risk Management Committee meets quarterly and reviews the
enterprise risk register, key risk indicators (KRIs), and emerging risks.

CREDIT RISK
-----------
Credit risk is the most significant risk for Initech given its lending
operations. As of March 31, 2024:

- Gross loan portfolio: INR 32,000 crore
- Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA): INR 960 crore (3.0%)
- Net Non-Performing Assets (NNPA): INR 384 crore (1.2%)
- Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR): 68%
- Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR): 18.2% (regulatory minimum: 15%)

Credit risk is managed through:
- Centralized credit underwriting with automated scoring models
- Portfolio concentration limits (no single borrower exceeding 2%
  of total portfolio)
- Sector-wise exposure limits
- Regular portfolio stress testing under adverse macroeconomic scenarios
- Early warning system for detecting signs of borrower distress

MARKET RISK
-----------
Market risk arises from adverse movements in interest rates, foreign
exchange rates, and equity prices.

Interest Rate Risk:
Initech's asset-liability gap shows a negative duration gap of 0.8 years,
meaning the company is moderately exposed to rising interest rates. A
parallel upward shift of 100 basis points in the yield curve would reduce
the economic value of equity by approximately INR 280 crore.

The company uses interest rate swaps and forward rate agreements to
manage this exposure. The Treasury Committee meets weekly to review
interest rate positions.

Foreign Exchange Risk:
Foreign exchange exposure is limited as 95% of operations are
India-denominated. However, the company has foreign currency borrowings
of USD 150 million (INR 1,250 crore equivalent) from international
development finance institutions. These borrowings are fully hedged
through cross-currency swaps.

OPERATIONAL RISK
-----------------
Operational risk includes the risk of loss resulting from inadequate
or failed internal processes, people, systems, or external events.

Key operational risk areas:

1. TECHNOLOGY AND CYBER RISK: Initech's digital banking platform serves
   12 million customers. The company faces risks from cyber attacks,
   system outages, and data breaches. Mitigation measures include:
   - 24/7 Security Operations Centre (SOC)
   - Annual ISO 27001 certification
   - Quarterly VAPT (Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing)
   - Business continuity plans with RPO of 4 hours and RTO of 8 hours

2. FRAUD RISK: Lending operations are exposed to fraud risk including
   identity fraud, document forgery, and collusion. The company deployed
   an AI-based fraud detection system in FY 2024 that flagged 2,400
   suspicious applications, of which 1,800 were confirmed fraudulent,
   preventing estimated losses of INR 45 crore.

3. COMPLIANCE AND REGULATORY RISK: As a regulated financial institution,
   Initech must comply with RBI regulations, SEBI requirements, and
   various state-level regulations. Non-compliance can result in
   monetary penalties, licence restrictions, or reputational damage.
   The company has a dedicated compliance team of 35 professionals.

4. PEOPLE RISK: Key person dependencies, inadequate staffing, and
   employee misconduct are ongoing operational risks. Succession
   planning covers all CXO and CXO-1 positions.

LIQUIDITY RISK
--------------
Liquidity risk is the risk that the company may not be able to meet
its financial obligations as they fall due.

Initech maintains a Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) of 142% against a
regulatory minimum of 100%. The company holds high-quality liquid assets
(HQLA) of INR 5,800 crore, including government securities and AAA-rated
corporate bonds.

Funding sources:
- Customer deposits: INR 21,000 crore (65% of funding mix)
- Bank borrowings: INR 6,500 crore (20%)
- Bond issuances: INR 3,200 crore (10%)
- Subordinated debt: INR 1,600 crore (5%)

The company conducts monthly liquidity stress tests simulating scenarios
including deposit run-off, funding market freeze, and asset fire sales.

EMERGING RISKS
--------------
The Board has identified the following emerging risks that may become
material over the next 2–3 years:

1. CLIMATE RISK: Physical risks (extreme weather events affecting
   borrowers' ability to repay) and transition risks (regulatory changes
   related to climate disclosures and green finance requirements).

2. AI AND MODEL RISK: Increasing reliance on AI-based models for credit
   scoring and fraud detection introduces model risk including bias,
   interpretability challenges, and regulatory scrutiny.

3. FINTECH COMPETITION: Digital-first lending platforms are increasing
   competitive pressure on pricing and customer acquisition.
