COMPANY OVERVIEW
----------------
ACME Corporation is a diversified technology and manufacturing conglomerate
headquartered in Mumbai, India. Founded in 1998, the company operates across
three primary business segments: Enterprise Software Solutions, Industrial
Automation, and Digital Financial Services.

As of March 31, 2024, ACME Corporation employs approximately 45,000 people
across 12 countries, with major operations in India, the United States,
the United Kingdom, and Singapore.

FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS — FY 2024
------------------------------
Total Revenue: INR 18,450 crore (USD 2.21 billion), an increase of 14.2%
year-over-year from INR 16,155 crore in FY 2023.

Operating Profit (EBITDA): INR 3,690 crore, representing an EBITDA margin
of 20.0%, compared to 18.5% in the prior year.

Net Profit After Tax: INR 2,214 crore, a growth of 19.8% from INR 1,848
crore in FY 2023.

Earnings Per Share (EPS): INR 44.28, up from INR 36.96 in FY 2023.

Total Dividend: INR 12 per share (interim INR 5 + final INR 7),
representing a payout ratio of 27.1%.

Cash and Cash Equivalents: INR 4,200 crore as of March 31, 2024.

Total Debt: INR 2,800 crore, resulting in a net debt-to-equity ratio of 0.12.

SEGMENT PERFORMANCE
-------------------
Enterprise Software Solutions:
Revenue of INR 8,900 crore (+16.5% YoY), driven by strong demand for
cloud migration services and AI-powered analytics platforms. This segment
contributed 48.2% of total revenue. Key clients include three Fortune 500
companies in the banking and insurance sector.

Industrial Automation:
Revenue of INR 5,200 crore (+10.1% YoY). Growth was supported by
government infrastructure spending and the Production Linked Incentive (PLI)
scheme. The segment secured orders worth INR 6,800 crore for delivery
in FY 2025–2026.

Digital Financial Services:
Revenue of INR 4,350 crore (+15.8% YoY). The company's digital payments
platform processed 2.1 billion transactions during the year. Monthly
active users crossed 35 million.

STRATEGIC PRIORITIES
--------------------
1. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning: ACME plans to invest INR
   1,200 crore over the next three years in AI research and development.
   The company has established an AI Centre of Excellence in Bengaluru
   with 500 dedicated researchers.

2. International Expansion: The company is targeting entry into the
   Southeast Asian market (Vietnam, Indonesia, Thailand) through
   strategic acquisitions and joint ventures. A budget of INR 800 crore
   has been allocated for M&A activities in FY 2025.

3. Sustainability: ACME has committed to achieving net-zero carbon
   emissions by 2035. In FY 2024, renewable energy sources accounted for
   42% of the company's total energy consumption, up from 31% in FY 2023.

4. Talent Development: The company launched a comprehensive upskilling
   program targeting 10,000 employees in cloud computing, cybersecurity,
   and generative AI skills.

RISK FACTORS
------------
The following risk factors may materially affect the company's business,
financial condition, or results of operations:

1. MACROECONOMIC RISK: Global economic slowdown, inflationary pressures,
   and geopolitical tensions (including trade restrictions) could reduce
   client spending on technology services and delay project timelines.

2. CURRENCY RISK: Approximately 62% of revenue is denominated in foreign
   currencies (primarily USD and GBP). Adverse currency fluctuations
   could impact reported revenue and margins. The company uses forward
   contracts to hedge 60–70% of expected foreign currency receivables.

3. REGULATORY AND COMPLIANCE RISK: Operations across 12 jurisdictions
   expose the company to evolving data protection regulations (including
   India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, GDPR, and CCPA).
   Non-compliance could result in significant penalties and reputational
   damage.

4. TECHNOLOGY OBSOLESCENCE RISK: Rapid technological change, particularly
   in AI and cloud computing, requires continuous investment in R&D. Failure
   to adapt could erode competitive positioning.

5. CYBERSECURITY RISK: As a technology company handling sensitive client
   data, ACME faces persistent cybersecurity threats including ransomware,
   phishing, and advanced persistent threats (APTs). The company maintains
   ISO 27001 certification and conducts quarterly penetration testing.

6. CLIENT CONCENTRATION RISK: The top 10 clients contributed 34% of total
   revenue in FY 2024. Loss of any major client could materially impact
   revenue.

7. TALENT ATTRITION RISK: The technology industry faces high employee
   turnover rates. ACME's attrition rate was 16.2% in FY 2024, down
   from 19.8% in FY 2023, but remains above the company's target of 14%.

CORPORATE GOVERNANCE
--------------------
The Board of Directors comprises 12 members, including 7 independent
directors. The Board met 8 times during FY 2024. All mandatory committees
(Audit, Nomination & Remuneration, Stakeholder Relationship, CSR) are
constituted and functioning as per SEBI LODR requirements.

The company spent INR 52 crore on Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)
activities during FY 2024, exceeding the statutory requirement of 2% of
average net profit.

OUTLOOK FOR FY 2025
-------------------
Management expects revenue growth of 12–15% in FY 2025, driven by:
- Continued momentum in cloud migration and AI services
- Ramp-up of large deals signed in Q4 FY 2024
- Entry into new geographies
- Growing digital payments adoption

EBITDA margins are expected to remain in the range of 19.5–20.5%.

The Board has approved a capital expenditure plan of INR 1,500 crore for
FY 2025, primarily for data center expansion and R&D infrastructure.
